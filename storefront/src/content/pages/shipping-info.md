## Your options

**Collect free from the workshop.** 252 General Beyers Street, Pretoria North, weekdays 08:00 to 17:00. We contact you when your order is ready.

**The Courier Guy, door-to-door.** Delivered to your address. The price depends on the size and weight of your parcel:

| Parcel | Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 kg | from R{{shipping.tcg_bands.0.price_zar}} |
| Up to 5 kg | R{{shipping.tcg_bands.1.price_zar}} |
| Larger or heavier (for example batteries) | R{{shipping.tcg_bands.2.price_zar}} |

**Pudo locker.** Small parcels only (up to {{shipping.pudo_max_weight_g}} g), R{{shipping.pudo_locker_price_zar}}. You collect from a locker near you at any hour. Heavier orders cannot go to a locker, so the option disappears at checkout.

## Free delivery

Orders of **R{{shipping.free_shipping_threshold_zar}} or more** are delivered free, for parcels up to 5 kg. Large and heavy items (batteries and other bulky gear) always carry the freight fee.

## How long it takes

We prepare your order within {{production_lead_time_days}} after your payment is confirmed. Courier time is then about 2 to 5 working days (longer for remote areas), or 2 to 4 days to a Pudo locker. You get a tracking link by email.

## Good to know

- We deliver within South Africa only.
- Some products are sent directly by our supplier after payment.
- Full details, including failed deliveries and damaged parcels, are in our [shipping policy](/shop/legal/shipping-policy).
