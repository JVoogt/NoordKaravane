Noord Karavane is a specialist **caravan and trailer workshop** at 252 General Beyers Street in Pretoria North. We service, repair and upgrade caravans every day: wheel bearings, brakes, electrics, solar, water systems, awnings and the odd rescue job. We are an authorised Kampi agent, and we also run secure caravan storage (KaravaanHub).

## Why a shop

The things we fit are the things people ask us where to buy. Lithium batteries that actually suit a caravan. Lights that survive the corrugations. Towing cameras, water pumps, first aid kits, braai gear and the small things that make a campsite work. This shop puts the gear we trust in one place, at fair prices, with people who know caravans behind it.

## What you get

- **Advice from the workshop.** Not sure a battery or pump suits your setup? Ask us before you buy.
- **Fitting.** Buy it here and we can fit it at the workshop. [Book via the contact page](/shop/contact).
