Ordering takes a few minutes, and you do not need a card.

## 1. Choose your products

Browse the [shop](/shop/), pick any options (for example size or capacity), and add to your cart. Not sure it suits your caravan or vehicle? [Ask us first](/shop/contact).

## 2. Check out

Enter your details and choose how you want to get your order: **courier to your door**, **Pudo locker** (small parcels), or **collect free** from the workshop in Pretoria North. You see the full price, including delivery, before you place the order.

## 3. Pay by EFT

After you place the order we show you our banking details and your **payment reference** (like NK1042). We also email them to you. Pay from your banking app within **{{eft_payment_window_hours}} hours** and use the reference exactly as shown.

Your order is only released for preparation once the money reflects in our account. This can take up to a business day if you bank with a different bank than ours. Sending proof of payment to {{email_orders}} speeds things up. If we do not receive payment within {{eft_payment_window_hours}} hours the order is cancelled automatically (nothing is charged).

## 4. We get it ready

We confirm your payment by email and prepare your order. Allow {{production_lead_time_days}}. Some items are sent directly by our supplier, and we will tell you if that applies.

## 5. Delivery or collection

- **Courier or Pudo:** you get a tracking link by email when it ships. See [delivery details](/shop/shipping).
- **Collection:** we contact you when it is ready at 252 General Beyers Street, Mon to Thu 08:00 to 16:00, Fri 08:00 to 14:00. Bring your order reference.

## Need to change something?

Email {{email_orders}} or WhatsApp +{{whatsapp}} with your order number as soon as possible. Our [returns policy](/shop/legal/returns) covers changes of mind and faulty goods.
