## How do I pay?

By EFT. Place your order, and we show you our banking details and your reference straight away (and email them). Pay from your banking app within {{eft_payment_window_hours}} hours. We get your order ready once the money reflects. There are no card fees and we never see your card details.

## How long does delivery take?

We prepare orders within {{production_lead_time_days}} after payment is confirmed. The courier then takes about 2 to 5 working days (longer for remote areas). You get a tracking link by email.

## Can I collect my order?

Yes, free. Choose collection at checkout and we will contact you when it is ready at 252 General Beyers Street, Pretoria North (Mon to Thu 08:00 to 16:00, Fri 08:00 to 14:00). Bring your order reference.

## How much is delivery?

It depends on parcel size: from R{{shipping.tcg_bands.0.price_zar}} for small parcels up to R{{shipping.tcg_bands.2.price_zar}} for bulky items like batteries. Orders of R{{shipping.free_shipping_threshold_zar}} or more are delivered free (parcels up to 5 kg). You see the exact fee at checkout before you pay.

## Why is the Pudo locker option missing?

Lockers only take small parcels (up to {{shipping.pudo_max_weight_g}} g). If your order is bigger, choose The Courier Guy or collect from us.

## Can you fit it for me?

Yes. We are a caravan workshop, so we can fit batteries, cameras, lighting, pumps and more. [Contact us](/shop/contact) to book a slot, or mention it in your message.

## How do I know a product suits my caravan?

Check the product page, and if you are unsure, ask. Send us your caravan make and model on WhatsApp (+{{whatsapp}}) or through the [contact page](/shop/contact) and we will tell you honestly whether it fits and works.

## Do the products come with a warranty?

Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act apply to everything we sell, and many products also have the manufacturer's warranty (for example lithium batteries and electronics). Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase. See [refunds and returns](/shop/legal/returns).

## What if I change my mind?

You have 7 days from receiving your order to cancel without giving a reason (ECTA section 44). Return the item unused in its original packaging. See [refunds and returns](/shop/legal/returns).

## Some items say they are sent by the supplier. What does that mean?

For some products (for example first aid kits from The First Responders), the supplier packs and ships directly to you once we confirm your payment. You order and pay through us, and we handle any problems.

## Is VAT included?

Yes. Prices include VAT. We are VAT registered (VAT no. {{vat_number}}) and your order confirmation serves as your record.
