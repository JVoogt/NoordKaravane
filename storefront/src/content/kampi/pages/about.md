Kampi started with a simple idea: more people out camping, fewer caravans standing still in the garage. Kampi Store is the gear side of that. It holds the kit renters and hosts kept asking about, in one place, at fair prices.

<p data-seller>Every order is sold and fulfilled by {{legal_name}} ({{brand}}), a specialist caravan and trailer workshop in Pretoria North. They pack and send your gear, answer your questions and handle returns. The Kampi rental platform at kampi.co.za is a separate business.</p>

## What you will find here

Lithium batteries that suit a caravan. Lights that survive the corrugations. Fans, braai gear, first aid kits, water pumps, towing cameras and the small things that make a campsite work. Each product was picked because it earns its place on a trip.

## What you get

- **Advice from caravan people.** Not sure a battery or pump suits your setup? [Ask before you buy](/contact).
- **Fitting.** The workshop that ships your order can also fit it in Pretoria North. [Ask about fitting](/contact?topic=fitting).
- **Easy payment.** Pay by EFT from your banking app. No card details needed. [How ordering works](/how-to-order).
- **Delivery or collection.** Courier countrywide, free over {{zar:shipping.free_shipping_threshold_zar}} for parcels up to 5 kg, or collect free in Pretoria North. [Delivery details](/shipping).
