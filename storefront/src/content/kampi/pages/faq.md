## Who sells the gear on Kampi Store?

<span data-seller>Every order is sold and fulfilled by {{legal_name}} ({{brand}}), a caravan workshop in Pretoria North.</span> They take your payment, pack and send your order, and handle returns. That is also why your order number starts with NK.

## How do I pay?

By EFT. Place your order, and we show you the banking details and your reference straight away (and email them). Pay from your banking app within {{eft_payment_window_hours}} hours. We get your order ready once the money reflects. There are no card fees and nobody sees your card details.

## I have a Kampi code. How do I use it?

Type it into "Have a code?" in your cart or at checkout. A code works once, on one order, and some need a minimum spend. The store takes one code per order.

## How long does delivery take?

We prepare orders within {{production_lead_time_days}} after payment is confirmed. The courier then takes about 2 to 5 working days (longer for remote areas). You get a tracking link by email.

## Can I collect my order?

Yes, free. Choose collection at checkout and we will contact you when it is ready at {{workshop.address_1}}, {{workshop.city}} (Mon to Thu 08:00 to 16:00, Fri 08:00 to 14:00). Bring your order reference.

## How much is delivery?

It depends on parcel size: from {{zar:shipping.tcg_bands.0.price_zar}} for small parcels up to {{zar:shipping.tcg_bands.2.price_zar}} for bulky items like batteries. Orders of {{zar:shipping.free_shipping_threshold_zar}} or more are delivered free (parcels up to 5 kg). You see the exact fee at checkout before you pay.

## Why is the Pudo locker option missing?

Lockers only take small parcels (up to {{shipping.pudo_max_weight_g}} g). If your order is bigger, choose The Courier Guy or collect in Pretoria North.

## Can you fit it for me?

Yes. The workshop that ships your order can fit batteries, cameras, lighting, pumps and more in Pretoria North. [Ask about fitting](/contact?topic=fitting).

## How do I know a product suits my caravan?

Check the product page, and if you are unsure, ask. Send your caravan make and model through the [contact page](/contact) and you get an honest answer on whether it fits and works.

## Do the products come with a warranty?

Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act apply to everything on the store, and many products also have the manufacturer's warranty (for example lithium batteries and electronics). Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase. See [refunds and returns](/legal/returns).

## What if I change my mind?

You have 7 days from receiving your order to cancel without giving a reason (ECTA section 44). Return the item unused in its original packaging. See [refunds and returns](/legal/returns).

## Some items say they are sent by the supplier. What does that mean?

For some products (for example first aid kits from The First Responders), the supplier packs and ships directly to you once your payment is confirmed. You order and pay through Kampi Store, and any problem is handled for you.

## Is VAT included?

Yes. Prices include VAT (VAT no. {{vat_number}}) and your order confirmation serves as your record.
