## Your options

**Collect free in Pretoria North.** {{workshop.address_1}}, {{workshop.city}}, Mon to Thu 08:00 to 16:00, Fri 08:00 to 14:00. You are contacted when your order is ready.

**The Courier Guy, door-to-door.** Delivered to your address. The price depends on the size and weight of your parcel:

| Parcel | Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 kg | from {{zar:shipping.tcg_bands.0.price_zar}} |
| Up to 5 kg | {{zar:shipping.tcg_bands.1.price_zar}} |
| Larger or heavier (for example batteries) | {{zar:shipping.tcg_bands.2.price_zar}} |

**Pudo locker.** Small parcels only (up to {{shipping.pudo_max_weight_g}} g), {{zar:shipping.pudo_locker_price_zar}}. You collect from a locker near you at any hour. Heavier orders cannot go to a locker, so the option disappears at checkout.

## Free delivery

Orders of **{{zar:shipping.free_shipping_threshold_zar}} or more** are delivered free, for parcels up to 5 kg. Large and heavy items (batteries and other bulky gear) always carry the freight fee.

## How long it takes

Your order is prepared within {{production_lead_time_days}} after your payment is confirmed. Courier time is then about 2 to 5 working days (longer for remote areas), or 2 to 4 days to a Pudo locker. You get a tracking link by email.

## Good to know

- Delivery is within South Africa only.
- Some products are sent directly by the supplier after payment.
- Full details, including failed deliveries and damaged parcels, are in the [shipping policy](/legal/shipping-policy).
