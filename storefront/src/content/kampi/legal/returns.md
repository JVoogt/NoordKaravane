We want you to be happy with what you buy. If something is wrong, or you change your mind, here is what happens. Returns, refunds and repairs are handled by <span data-seller>{{legal_name}}</span>, the seller.

## 1. If your product is faulty or not as described

Under section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA), goods must be of good quality, in good working order and free of defects. If you find a defect within **6 months** of delivery, you may choose to have the item **repaired, replaced, or refunded**. Some products also carry the manufacturer's own warranty (for example lithium batteries and electronics). Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase, because we will help you claim under it.

- **We pay** the cost of returning a faulty or wrong item and of sending the replacement.
- Please tell us as soon as you notice the fault. Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, incorrect installation by someone other than us, and damage you caused are not defects.

## 2. Changing your mind: 7-day cooling-off (ECTA s44)

Because you buy online, section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA) lets you cancel without giving a reason and without penalty within **7 days of receiving your order**.

To use it:

- Tell us (email {{email_orders}}) **within 7 days** of receiving the goods that you are cancelling, then send the goods back to us promptly.
- Return the item **unused, in its original packaging with all accessories**, so we can resell it. You may unpack and inspect it as you would in a shop, but you remain responsible for it while it is in your care, and we may claim for loss in value caused by use beyond that. Items that have been installed or used (for example a battery that has been charged and fitted) may have lost value.
- **You pay the direct cost of returning** the goods under the cooling-off. You are not charged a penalty or anything else for cancelling. You can also return the goods in person at our workshop in Pretoria North.

If you have a statutory right to cancel, you may not waive it, and nothing in this policy limits it.

## 3. How to return something

1. Email {{email_orders}} with your **order number** (for example NK1042), what is wrong or why you are returning it, and for faults **clear photos** of the issue.
2. We will reply with return instructions and the return address. Do not send anything back before you hear from us, so it does not go missing. Some supplier-shipped items (for example first aid kits) are returned to the supplier. We will tell you.
3. Pack the item securely and send it with a tracked service, or drop it at our workshop. Keep your proof of postage.
4. When it reaches us and passes a quick check, we process your refund, replacement or repair.

## 4. Refunds

- Refunds are paid by **EFT to the bank account you give us**, in the name on the order wherever possible.
- For a cooling-off cancellation we refund in full within **30 days** of the date you cancel (ECTA s44), and usually much sooner. For faulty goods we refund promptly after we accept the claim.
- The refund covers the price of the item(s) and, for a faulty or incorrect item or a full cancellation, the delivery fee you paid. The only cost you bear for a cooling-off return is the direct cost of returning the goods.
- If your order was **unpaid and cancelled** after {{eft_payment_window_hours}} hours, nothing is due, so nothing needs refunding.

## 5. Cancelling before we ship

Tell us as early as you can. If we have not shipped or fitted your order, we cancel and refund in full.

## 6. Exchanges

We can exchange for a different model or size subject to availability. Contact us within 7 days of delivery. The item must be in the condition described under cooling-off. We will confirm any price difference before we start.
