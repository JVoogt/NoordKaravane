## 2. What we collect, why, and our lawful basis

| What | Why | Lawful basis (POPIA s11) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone, delivery address, locker choice | To take, prepare, deliver and support your order | Needed to conclude and perform the contract with you |
| Order history, payment reference, proof of EFT payment | To confirm payment, issue invoices, handle returns and keep tax records | Contract; legal obligation (tax and consumer law) |
| A discount code you use, and the order it was used on | To apply the discount and stop a code being used twice | Contract |
| Messages you send us (email, contact form) | To reply and keep a record of the conversation | Legitimate interest; contract |
| Website usage data (pages viewed, device, approximate location, cart events) via Google Analytics 4 | To understand and improve the store | Your consent (cookie banner) |
| Technical and security data (IP address, browser, request logs, bot-check signals) | To keep the site secure and prevent fraud and abuse | Legitimate interest |

We collect information directly from you (and from your browser when you use the site). We only collect what we need.

Supplying your information is voluntary, but if you do not give us your name, delivery details and contact details we cannot take or deliver your order. Analytics information is entirely optional. Some laws require us to keep certain records once you order: the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 and the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962 (financial and tax records), and the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (records of the transaction).

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** send marketing emails from Kampi Store. We do **not** make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

## 3. Kampi vouchers

Some discount codes are issued to Kampi renters and hosts by the Kampi rental platform (for example after a review or a competition). For those codes we keep only a scrambled form (a one-way hash) of the email address the code was issued to, never the address itself. The platform receives counts only: how many of its codes were issued and how many were used. It does not receive your name, contact details, address or what you bought.

## 5. Cross-border transfers (POPIA s72)

Some of our providers (Cloudflare, Google, Brevo, Microsoft Azure) process information outside South Africa, including in the EU and the United States. We only transfer information where the recipient is subject to law, binding corporate rules or an agreement that gives substantially similar protection to POPIA, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you, or where you have consented (for example, by accepting analytics cookies).

## 6. How long we keep it

A summary: order and invoice records 5 years (tax law); abandoned carts 30 days; server and security logs 90 days; backups 30 days; analytics 14 months. The full schedule is in our [Data Retention Policy](/legal/data-retention/). When retention ends we delete or de-identify the information.

## 7. How we protect it

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and two-factor protection on the admin area, least-privilege access, hashed passwords, patched and isolated containers, and encrypted backups. Details are in the [Data Retention Policy](/legal/data-retention/). If there is a security compromise affecting you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA s22 requires.

## 9. Your rights

You may, free of charge for the request itself:

- ask whether we hold information about you and **access** it (a reasonable prescribed fee may apply to copies of records requested under PAIA);
- ask us to **correct** or **delete** information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (this does not affect processing already done);
- ask for access to records under the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA). Send PAIA requests to our Information Officer at {{email_privacy}}.

Email {{email_privacy}} with your name and the email address used on your order. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days.
