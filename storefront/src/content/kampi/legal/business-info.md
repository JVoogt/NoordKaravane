## The Kampi name

Kampi Store uses the Kampi name and logo with the permission of Kampi, which runs the caravan rental platform at kampi.co.za. The platform is a separate business. It does not sell, deliver or guarantee the goods on this store and is not a party to your purchase.

## Cancellation, returns and refunds

You have a 7-day cooling-off right under section 44 of ECTA (you may cancel without giving a reason or paying a penalty within 7 days after you receive the goods) and rights for faulty goods under the Consumer Protection Act. The return, exchange and refund policy is in [Refunds and Returns](/legal/returns/).

## Terms of the agreement and your record of the transaction

The terms that apply to your order are our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms/), [Shipping Policy](/legal/shipping-policy/) and [Refunds and Returns](/legal/returns/) policy. They are web pages that you can read, print, save or download from your browser at any time, and we email you your order confirmation so you can keep a copy with them. There is no guarantee beyond your rights under the law. Each order is a one-off sale, not an ongoing agreement, so there is no minimum duration.

## Your rights and complaints

Our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms/) explain how your order is concluded and how to complain. You may also approach the National Consumer Commission or a consumer ombud. You can review your order before you submit it and correct mistakes, and you will get an order confirmation by email. We keep your order record for 5 years and you can ask us at {{email_orders}} for a copy at any time during that period.

We do not subscribe to an external code of conduct or to an alternative dispute resolution code at this time. Our policies on this site set out how we operate. If we cannot resolve a complaint, you may approach the National Consumer Commission or an accredited consumer goods ombud.
