We deliver within **South Africa only**, or you can **collect free** from the seller's workshop in Pretoria North.

## 1. How long it takes

We prepare your order once your payment is confirmed. Allow **{{production_lead_time_days}}** from the day we confirm payment, then courier time is added. The clock does not start while an EFT is unconfirmed. Items we have to order in from a supplier may take longer, and we will tell you if that applies to your order. Public holidays and courier delays can add time.

## 3. Pudo locker orders

Pudo lockers take small parcels only. If your order is too big for a locker, that option is not offered at checkout. Choose your preferred locker and give us the exact locker name or code (use the Pudo locker finder). You will get an SMS or email from Pudo with a collection code when your parcel is ready. Lockers hold parcels for a limited time (currently around 7 days, check the Pudo notification). Please collect before it expires.

## 4. Collection from the workshop

Choose collection at checkout. We will contact you when your order is ready. Collect at **{{workshop.address_1}}, {{workshop.city}}, Mon to Thu 08:00 to 16:00, Fri 08:00 to 14:00** and bring your order reference. If you also want something fitted, tell us and we will book a slot.

## 5. Items shipped directly by our suppliers

Some products (for example first aid kits from The First Responders) are sent to you directly by the supplier once your payment is confirmed. You will still get your order confirmation and tracking details from us or the supplier.

## 6. Tracking

When your order ships we email you a tracking link for The Courier Guy or Pudo. Keep an eye on your inbox and spam folder.

## 7. Failed delivery and uncollected parcels

- **Door-to-door:** the courier will usually attempt delivery more than once and contact you. Make sure your address, complex or building details and phone number are right and that someone can receive the parcel.
- **Locker:** if you do not collect before expiry, the parcel goes back to the courier and then to us.
- If a parcel comes back to us because of a wrong address or non-collection, we will contact you. We can resend once you pay the delivery fee again. If you do not respond within 30 days we may cancel the order and refund the goods price less the delivery costs we incurred.

## 8. Risk, damage and missing parcels

Risk passes to you on delivery (or on collection). Check your parcel when you receive it. If it arrives damaged, or tracking shows delivered but you did not get it, email {{email_orders}} within 5 working days with your order number and photos, and we will sort it out with the courier. Your rights for defective goods under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected.

## 9. Address changes

Email {{email_orders}} as soon as possible. We can change the address only before the order is handed to the courier.
