## 2. What we collect, why, and our lawful basis

| What | Why | Lawful basis (POPIA s11) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone, delivery address, locker choice | To take, make, pay for, deliver and support your order | Needed to conclude and perform the contract with you |
| Order history, payment reference, proof of EFT payment | To confirm payment, issue invoices, handle returns and keep tax records | Contract; legal obligation (tax and consumer law) |
| Account details (email, hashed password), if you create an account | To let you view orders and check out faster | Contract / your consent |
| Messages you send us (email, WhatsApp, contact form) | To reply and keep a record of the conversation | Legitimate interest; contract |
| Marketing details (email, first name) and consent record | To send you news, drops and offers, only if you opt in | Your consent (POPIA s69) |
| Website usage data (pages viewed, device, approximate location, cart events) via Google Analytics | To understand and improve the site | Your consent (cookie banner) |
| Advertising identifiers (Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API: hashed email/phone, order value, browser IDs `_fbp`/`_fbc`) | To measure and improve our ads and show relevant ads | Your consent (cookie banner and the marketing tickbox at checkout) |
| Technical and security data (IP address, browser, request logs, bot-check signals) | To keep the site secure and prevent fraud and abuse | Legitimate interest |

We collect information directly from you (and from your browser when you use the site). We do not collect it from other sources. We only collect what we need.

Supplying your information is voluntary, but if you do not give us your name, delivery details and contact details we cannot take or deliver your order. Marketing, analytics and advertising information is entirely optional. Some laws require us to keep certain records once you order: the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 and the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962 (financial and tax records), and the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (records of the transaction).

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

## 4. Cross-border transfers (POPIA s72)

Some of our providers (Cloudflare, Google, Meta, Brevo, Microsoft Azure) process information outside South Africa, including in the EU and the United States. We only transfer information where the recipient is subject to law, binding corporate rules or an agreement that gives substantially similar protection to POPIA, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you, or where you have consented (for example, by accepting analytics and marketing cookies).

## 5. How long we keep it

A summary: order and invoice records 5 years (tax law); marketing list until you unsubscribe; abandoned carts 30 days; server and security logs 90 days; backups 30 days; analytics 14 months. The full schedule is in our [Data Retention Policy](/shop/legal/data-retention/). When retention ends we delete or de-identify the information.

## 6. How we protect it

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and two-factor protection on the admin area, least-privilege access, hashed passwords, patched and isolated containers, and encrypted backups. Details are in the [Data Retention Policy](/shop/legal/data-retention/). If there is a security compromise affecting you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA s22 requires.

## 7. Direct marketing (POPIA s69)

We send marketing emails only if you opted in (the checkbox at checkout is unticked by default, or you joined our list on the site). Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email {{email_privacy}}. Unsubscribing does not stop service messages about an order you have placed.

## 9. Your rights

You may, free of charge for the request itself:

- ask whether we hold information about you and **access** it (a reasonable prescribed fee may apply to copies of records requested under PAIA);
- ask us to **correct** or **delete** information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (this does not affect processing already done);
- ask for access to records under the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA). Send PAIA requests to our Information Officer at {{email_privacy}}.

Email {{email_privacy}} with your name and the email address used on your order. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days.
