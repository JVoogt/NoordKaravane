Cookies are small files saved on your device. We use as few as possible. **Analytics and marketing cookies stay off until you say yes.**

## Categories

- **Necessary:** needed for the shop to work and stay secure. Always on.
- **Analytics:** help us understand how the site is used (Google Analytics 4). Off unless you accept.
- **Marketing:** let Meta measure ads and show relevant ones (Meta Pixel). Off unless you accept.

We use Google Consent Mode v2: until you consent, Google tags run with analytics and ad storage denied, and no analytics or advertising cookies are set.

## Cookies we use

| Cookie | Provider | Category | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cc_cookie` | Noord Karavane | Necessary | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |
| `nk_cart` | Noord Karavane | Necessary | Keeps your basket between pages | Up to 30 days |
| `__cf_bm` | Cloudflare | Necessary | Bot management and security | 30 minutes |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Necessary | Remembers that you passed a security check | Up to 1 year |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Analytics | Distinguishes users (pseudonymous ID) | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` (`_ga_*`) | Google Analytics | Analytics | Keeps session state | 2 years |
| `_fbp` | Meta | Marketing | Identifies browsers for ad measurement | 3 months |
| `_fbc` | Meta | Marketing | Stores the ad click that brought you here | 3 months |

Exact names and durations can be changed by those providers. If you spot a cookie not listed, tell us at {{email_privacy}}.

When you buy and have agreed to marketing, we may also send order events (hashed email or phone, order value) to Meta from our server. See the [Privacy Policy](/shop/legal/privacy/).

## Your choices

- Use the banner when you first visit, or click **Cookie preferences** in the footer at any time to change or withdraw consent. Withdrawing is as easy as giving it.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though necessary cookies are needed for checkout.
- Google offers an opt-out add-on for Analytics, and Meta lets you manage ad preferences in your account.
