## Cancellation, returns and refunds

You have a 7-day cooling-off right under section 44 of ECTA (you may cancel without giving a reason or paying a penalty within 7 days after you receive the goods) and rights for faulty goods under the Consumer Protection Act. Our return, exchange and refund policy is in [Refunds and Returns](/shop/legal/returns/).

## Terms of the agreement and your record of the transaction

The terms that apply to your order are our [Terms and Conditions](/shop/legal/terms/), [Shipping Policy](/shop/legal/shipping-policy/) and [Refunds and Returns](/shop/legal/returns/) policy.
