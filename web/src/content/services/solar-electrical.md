## What to send for a quote

- What you want to run: fridge, lights, water pump, fans, anything on 220 V
- How many nights you camp without a power point
- The battery and solar you have now, if any
- A few photos of the current setup and the roof

## Electrical faults we fix

- Lights that flicker or die
- A battery that's flat by the second night
- 220 V that stops working on a campsite plug
- Old or messy 12 V wiring that needs redoing, up to a full 12 V or 220 V rewire

Every [caravan service](/services/caravan-service/) also tests the battery, plugs, switches and 12 V electrics.
