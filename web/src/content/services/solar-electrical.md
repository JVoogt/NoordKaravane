## What to send for a quote

- What you want to run: fridge, lights, water pump, fans, anything on 220 V
- How many nights you camp without a power point
- The battery and solar you have now, if any
- A few photos of the current setup and the roof

## Charging while you drive and while you camp

Two parts keep a caravan or 4x4 camping battery full:

- **An MPPT solar charge controller** sits between the panel and the battery and gets the most out of the panel through the day.
- **A DC-DC charger** charges the camping battery from the tow vehicle's alternator while you drive. A lithium battery should have one, and so should any battery behind a newer vehicle with a smart alternator.

If you camp without a power point for more than a weekend, you'll want both.

## Renogy or Victron?

We fit both. Both are good, and they suit different setups.

- **Renogy** puts the DC-DC charger and the MPPT controller in one unit, from 30 A to 50 A. That's one box, less wiring and a lower price. It suits a weekend caravan, or one second battery in a 4x4.
- **Victron** keeps them as separate units: a SmartSolar MPPT controller and an Orion DC-DC charger. The SmartSolar controllers and the Orion Smart and Orion XS chargers have Bluetooth built in, so you watch and set them from the VictronConnect app, and you can add to the system later. It suits long trips, bigger battery banks and anyone who likes to see the numbers.

Not sure which? Send us what you run and how long you stay out, and we'll recommend one and quote it supplied and fitted. You can also see the [chargers and controllers in our shop](/shop/category/charge-controllers-monitoring/).

## Electrical faults we fix

- Lights that flicker or die
- A battery that's flat by the second night
- 220 V that stops working on a campsite plug
- Old or messy 12 V wiring that needs redoing, up to a full 12 V or 220 V rewire
