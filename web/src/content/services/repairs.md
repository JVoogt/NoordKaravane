## Bearings, brakes and chassis

Watch for a wheel that grinds or hums, a hub too hot to touch after a drive, brakes that pull to one side, or a caravan that sits low on one side.

We repair wheel bearings, brakes, suspension, leaf springs, couplers and jockey wheels, then test them before you tow away. A routine bearing repack is part of every [caravan service](/services/caravan-service/).

## Water systems and geysers

Typical signs: the pump runs but the taps stay dry, there's a damp patch near a tank, or the geyser won't heat.

We repair pumps, tanks, geysers and pipework, gas and electric, and pressure-test the system once the work is done.

## Woodrot

Look for a soft spot in the floor, a musty smell, swollen panels, or stains around windows and roof seams.

We restore floors, walls and frames to solid condition. Woodrot is quoted once we've seen it, because the damage behind the panel decides the size of the job.
