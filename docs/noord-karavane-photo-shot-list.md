# Photo shot list for the workshop pages

Date: 2026-11-08. For: the owner (Christiaan) or whoever has a phone in the workshop.

## Why

The repairs, electrical and solar, and recoveries pages are text only. The repo has one genuine workmanship photo, the wheel arch in `web/src/assets/lift-kit.jpg` (home hero and lift kits). It also has one genuine location photo, the painted wall in `web/src/assets/workshop-wall-sign.jpg`, now on Find us. No stock or AI images go on these pages: a photo there has to be real work from this workshop.

## How to shoot

- Phone camera, normal (1x) lens, daylight or the workshop lights on. No filters, no beauty mode.
- Landscape (horizontal) unless the line says portrait.
- No customer faces, number plates or names on job cards in the frame. Cover or blur them, or shoot around them.
- Send the originals (not WhatsApp-compressed) by email or a shared folder, with one line per photo saying what it is and the caravan make if you're happy for that to be named.

## After the photos arrive

Each photo gets a factual caption ("Wheel bearing and races cleaned before refitting") and plain alt text describing what is in the frame. Captions say only what the photo shows, so a single photo is never used as proof for a claim it does not show.
